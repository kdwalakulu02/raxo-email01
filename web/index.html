Why do you need Raxo Forms?

A simple form can introduce more challenges than you might expect. Keep enquiries, attachments and email delivery manageable.

Too many
unwanted messages?

Spam, bots and irrelevant submissions make genuine customer enquiries harder to find.

Suspicious
attachments?

Files submitted by unknown visitors may contain malicious or harmful content.

Backend setup
too complex?

Building and maintaining servers just to handle contact forms requires time and technical effort.

Exposed
email address?

Publishing your receiving email address in form code can lead to scraping and unwanted contact.

Key Features

Built for simpler, more private website form delivery.

Submission controls

Recipient verification, a honeypot field and sending limits help protect your form delivery.

Attachment Security

PDF files are scanned before forwarding. Flagged or unscannable attachments are withheld.

No Server Setup

Connect a standard HTML form without running your own backend or mail server.

Email Privacy

Keep your receiving email address out of public form code with a unique Form ID.

Verified Recipients

Verify your receiving address before form delivery starts.

Simple HTML Integration

Use an ordinary HTML form action with the generated endpoint.
